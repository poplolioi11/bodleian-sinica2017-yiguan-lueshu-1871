# 醫館略述(1871;Bodleian Sinica 2017) / Yi guan lue shu [Brief account of a medical institution]: Transcription dataset

Yi guan lue shu [Brief account of a medical institution] is an 1871 Chinese hospital publication, catalogued with [Foochow] as its place of publication and 柯為樑 (Dauphin William Osgood) credited with revision. Its Christian charitable purpose, hospital narrative, illustrations, donor lists and financial accounts support research on medical missions, local charitable networks and hospital publishing. Based on 21 images of Bodleian Sinica 2017, the dataset provides TXT, JSON and TEI XML for 37 designated physical faces, with uncertainties and source mappings. The provisional AI transcription includes selected source-image corrections; full human double collation and expert review have not been performed.
